# ThetaHealing (תטא הילינג): Foundations Research Report

**Scope:** History, worldview, cosmology (Seven Planes), core process ("road map"), vocabulary, and literature of the ThetaHealing® modality founded by Vianna Stibal. Compiled from the official ThetaHealing organization's materials, practitioner sites, Stibal's published books, book-trade metadata, and independent/skeptical references. Where sources disagree, this is flagged.

---

## 1. History and Origin

### 1.1 Vianna Stibal — founder biography (as told by the practice)

- **Vianna Stibal** (b. Idaho Falls, Idaho, USA) describes herself as an artist, writer, "intuitive reader," and self-styled naturopath. In the early 1990s she ran a practice in Idaho Falls doing massage, nutritional counseling, and psychic "readings."
- **The founding story (canonical version):** In **1995**, Stibal — then a mother of three young children — says she was diagnosed with a **cancer destroying her right femur** (variously described in her tellings as bone cancer/lymphoma in the leg; her leg is said to have swollen and "shrunk" by about three inches). She reports that conventional and alternative treatments failed, and that doctors discussed amputation.
- During a session in which she applied the same simple technique she used for intuitive readings — "going up out of her space" to connect with God/the Creator and *commanding* a healing while *witnessing* it done — she claims her leg was **instantaneously healed** and returned to its normal length. This event is presented as the empirical seed of the entire modality.
- She then began testing the technique on clients, concluded it was teachable, and set out to understand *why* it worked. Her answer: the practitioner drops into a **theta brainwave state** when "going up." The practice's lore says EEG measurements showed practitioners entering theta (4–7 Hz) during the process — this is the origin of the name.
- **Early names and publications:** The technique was initially taught under the name **"Orian Technique"** before being renamed ThetaHealing. Stibal self-published her first manuals — **"Go Up and Seek God"** (late 1990s) and **"Go Up and Work with God"** — through her own imprint (Rolling Thunder Publishing) before Hay House later republished her core works.

### 1.2 Institutionalization and timeline

| Year (approx.) | Milestone |

| 1995 | Claimed self-healing; technique developed out of Stibal's reading practice in Idaho Falls |
| Late 1990s | First self-published manuals (*Go Up and Seek God*, *Go Up and Work with God*); technique briefly called "Orian Technique" |

**Source disagreement:** Older official bios say Stibal "trained teachers and practitioners working in more than **25 countries**"; current marketing says **over 180 countries**. The diagnosis story is contested: in the 2015–2016 Idaho litigation, an expert who reviewed her medical records testified she was **never definitively diagnosed with cancer** (biopsies showed "suspicion of cancer, but were not diagnostic"), and her ex-husband testified the leg healed slowly, not instantly. The organization continues to teach the instant-healing narrative.

### 1.3 Corporate/trademark structure

- **"ThetaHealing" is a registered trademark** (also styled ThetaHealing®, Theta Healing) owned by Stibal's company, **Vianna's Nature Path Inc.** The mark is actively enforced: only practitioners and instructors certified through THInK-registered seminars may advertise using the name; instructor certification for each seminar level is obtainable **only from Vianna Stibal and her team** at THInK.
- The business model is a tiered seminar franchise: Certified Practitioner seminars (taught worldwide by certified instructors) → Instructor certifications (taught only by Vianna at THInK or at large international events) → honorary tiers ("**ThetaHealing Master**," "**Certificate of Science**") recognizing accumulation of all seminars.
- Practitioner path: **Basic DNA → Advanced DNA → Dig Deeper (+ You and the Creator)** are the foundational seminars; electives include Manifesting & Abundance (2 days), Intuitive Anatomy (15 days), World Relations (5 days), DNA 3 (5 days), Planes of Existence (5 days), Rhythm to a Perfect Weight, SoulMate, Wealth Consciousness, Growing Your Relationships/Family Ties, Animal seminar, Game of Life.

### 1.4 Presence in Israel

- ThetaHealing is **notably popular in Israel**; the official site maintains a dedicated Israel resources/locations page, and there is an organized Israeli ThetaHealing community ("ThetaHealing Israel / תטא הילינג ישראל") with active Facebook groups and a Google group dating back to the 2000s.
- The Israeli ThetaHealing organization produced the **Hebrew translation of the Basic ("יסוד") book**, and dozens of Israeli certified instructors (e.g., via ofenbari.co.il, thetaforlife.co.il, nogabenisrael.com, inbalhealing.com, rmalul.com, zafran.com, nitzayaniv.com) run the standard curriculum in Hebrew — typically a **3–4 day "קורס יסוד" (Basic DNA)** granting a practitioner certificate from "Vianna Stibal's international school," with the instructor track available only through Vianna's team. Vianna Stibal has held seminars for the Israeli market, and mainstream Israeli course-catalog sites (study.co.il) list ThetaHealing as an established complementary-therapy training track.

## 2. Core Worldview

### 2.1 The "Creator of All That Is" (בורא כל שיש)

- The central metaphysical object: an omnipresent, intelligent, loving **energy of creation** that "moves in all things and creates all things" — explicitly framed as **non-denominational** (identified with God, Buddha, Shiva, Goddess, Yahweh, Allah, Source, "the quantum field," etc., per the student's own religion). Stibal insists ThetaHealing "has no religion" but requires a belief in some creative force.
- Crucial doctrinal point: **the Creator does the healing, not the practitioner.** The practitioner's job is to connect, command/request, and **witness**. This distinguishes ThetaHealing (in its self-description) from modalities where the healer channels their own or intermediary energies — which ThetaHealing says carry "obligations" (see Planes 1–6).
- The Creator is located doctrinally at the **Seventh Plane of Existence**; connecting there gives "unconditional love" energy that is claimed to heal instantly, with "no obligations, no exchanges, no conditions."

### 2.2 The theta brainwave state — why it is central

- ThetaHealing claims that its signature visualization drops the practitioner into a **theta brainwave state (4–7 Hz)** — the frequency band EEG research associates with deep relaxation, hypnagogia, REM/dreaming, meditation, and (in hypnosis literature) reduced critical filtering and heightened suggestibility.
- The doctrine: theta is the "deep inner awareness" / "subconscious gateway" / "God-consciousness" state. In theta, (a) the **subconscious becomes directly editable** (hence belief work and downloads), and (b) the mind can **connect to the Creator** and co-create instant change. Stibal teaches that saying "go up and seek God / Creator" while visualizing rising through the crown "flips the brain into theta" measurably at the top of the head.
- Official materials cite informal EEG demonstrations that both practitioner *and client* enter theta during a session; independent replication does not exist.

### 2.3 Co-creation, commands, and free agency

- **Co-creation:** humans are "sparks of God" whose focused thought, in theta and joined to the Creator, participates in creating reality. Healing is a joint act: the practitioner *commands/requests*, the Creator *executes*, the practitioner *witnesses*.
- **The Command:** the operative speech-act. Doctrinal formula: *"Creator of All That Is, it is commanded/requested that [X] occur in the highest and best way. Thank you. It is done, it is done, it is done."* Teaching note: the **command is addressed to your own subconscious** (to bypass doubt); the **request is to the Creator** — "command" is not ordering God around but disciplining the practitioner's own limiting mind.
- **Witnessing:** the healing "is not complete until it is witnessed" — the practitioner must visually observe (in their mind's eye, inside the client's space) the Creator's energy completing the work. "Nothing happens in nature without being witnessed" is invoked.
- **Free agency / verbal permission:** a strict rule — no healing, reading, or belief change without the person's **verbal permission**; the client's subconscious can also refuse ("the healing was not accepted"), which doubles as the doctrine's explanation for failures.
- **Unconditional love as the agent:** the operative "force" is the Seventh-Plane energy of unconditional love; a single dose of it, witnessed into the body, is said to be able to change cells, beliefs, even (in Stibal's most extreme claims) regrow organs and limbs.
- **Power of words and thoughts:** thoughts/words are treated as having direct physical efficacy on the body (hence the obsession with wording of beliefs, programs, and commands).

## 3. The Seven Planes of Existence

The cosmological backbone of ThetaHealing, systematized in Stibal's book *Seven Planes of Existence* (Hay House, 2016) and the "Planes of Existence" seminar. All that exists is organized into seven "planes," each with its own energies, beings, knowledge — and, on planes one through six, **obligations/commitments** ("oaths, vows, and energy exchanges") that bind healers who draw power from them. The Seventh alone is free of obligations, which is why ThetaHealing insists on healing *only* through the Seventh Plane, while using the lower planes' knowledge "through the Seventh."

> **Source-order warning:** Some secondary summaries invert the numbering (calling the first plane the Creator). The canonical order in Stibal's books and the official curriculum is: 1 = minerals … 7 = Creator.

**First Plane — the Mineral Kingdom.** All inorganic, non-carbon matter: rocks, soil, crystals, minerals, the Earth itself "before organic life." Slowest/densest vibration. Teaches structure, stability, and the mineral substrate of the body (calcium, trace minerals). Healers who "work from" this plane: crystal healers, users of earth energies and ley lines, mineral remedies. Typical first-plane "obligation belief": healing must take a long time, or requires a crystal/talisman to work.

**Second Plane — the Plant Kingdom.** Organic, photosynthesis-based life: plants, trees, herbs, vitamins, and (in Stibal's cosmology) **fairies and nature elementals**, plus yeasts/molds. Teaches harmony with nature and the body's relationship with nutrients. Second-plane healers: herbalists, naturopaths, aromatherapists, those who commune with plant spirits. Obligation beliefs: "only herbs/vitamins heal," or debts to nature spirits.

**Third Plane — Animals and Humans.** Protein/carbon-based, mobile life. This is *our* plane: the plane of **illusion, ego, drama, and emotion**, governed by beliefs, fear, time, and "the reality of problem-solving." Its purpose: a school in which spiritual beings learn to master thoughts and emotions inside a body. Most disease, per the doctrine, is created here by programs held on the four belief levels. Third-plane healers work with the body directly (bodyworkers; conventional medicine is also often mapped here). Between the Third and Fourth Planes sits the holding zone of the **waywards** (see glossary).

**Fourth Plane — the Spirit World / Ancestors.** The "land of spirit": deceased **ancestors**, spirit guides, animal totems, many **guardian angels** (often ancestors on assignment), and the master **shamans**. It is the plane accessed by mediums, shamanic healers, and ancestor-venerating traditions. Teaches ancestral knowledge and that consciousness survives death; genetic belief work connects here (ancestral programs "held in the DNA"). Characteristic obligations Stibal warns about: initiation-through-illness, "you must give something up (an organ, a sense, a relationship) to gain healing power," spirits borrowing the healer's energy, sacrifice-based bargains.

**Fifth Plane — Angels and Ascended Masters.** The dualistic "heavens": **angels, archangels, ascended masters** (Christ, Buddha, and others who mastered the Third Plane), **Councils of Twelve**, heavenly hierarchies — and, on its lower degrees, the adversarial beings (fallen angels, "Legions"); it is the plane of the **battle between good and evil**. Souls are said to originate here ("fifth-plane beings having a third-plane experience"). Teaches mastery, co-creation, and love, but its obligations are bargaining and allegiance: worship, trades ("serve me and I'll heal you"), spiritual warfare, dualistic drama. Fifth-plane healers channel angels/masters or invoke divine intermediaries. ThetaHealing says masters *descend* from here to teach on the Third Plane.

**Sixth Plane — the Laws.** Pure institutional structure of the universe: the **Laws** — Law of Time, Law of Magnetism, Law of Gravity, Law of Electricity, Law of Cause and Effect (karma), Law of Compassion, Law of Pure Intent, Law of Truth, Law of Free Will, Law of Vibration/Attraction, Law of Nature, etc. — along with **tones, colors, numbers, sacred geometry, and the "music of the spheres."** Experienced in meditation as the "great void"/dark-matter-like **jelly-like substance** of shifting rainbow colors that the practitioner passes through en route to the Seventh Plane. Sixth-plane healers: numerologists, astrologers, sound healers, sacred-geometry workers; "virtue is the key to the Laws." Obligation: rigid rule-boundedness (e.g., belief that karma *must* be repaid through suffering).

**Seventh Plane — the Creator of All That Is.** Pure creative energy; the "Alpha and Omega" that **creates all the other planes**; experienced as **pearlescent, iridescent white light** and described as the plane that "just is." No beings, no duality, no obligations, no energy exchange — only unconditional love and instant creation. Doctrine: healing from the Seventh Plane is instantaneous, costless, and safe, and *through* it one can use the knowledge of all other planes without their bindings. The entire ThetaHealing method is defined as "the practice of connecting to the Seventh Plane."

**How practitioners use the planes:** (a) as a diagnostic taxonomy of *other* healing modalities and their hidden "contracts"; (b) in belief work, to find and revoke "oaths, vows, and obligations" a client allegedly carries toward planes 1–6 (e.g., a past-life shaman's vow of poverty); (c) as the map for the road-map meditation; (d) in the Planes of Existence seminar, to work with each plane's "healings of the seven planes" through Seventh-Plane connection.

## 4. The Basic "Road Map" — Going Up to the Seventh Plane

The signature guided visualization ("Road Map to All That Is"), read verbatim from Stibal's books in every Basic seminar. Steps as taught:

1. **Preparation.** Sit comfortably, feet flat, hands relaxed (thumb-and-finger optionally touching), close eyes, take a deep breath in. State an intention to connect to the Creator.
2. **Ground and gather energy.** Visualize your consciousness/energy going **down into the center of Mother Earth**, "the All That Is" of the Earth. Draw that energy **up through the soles of your feet**, up through the legs and body, **opening every chakra** as it rises.
3. **Exit through the crown.** In a "beautiful ball of light," send your consciousness up and **out of the crown chakra**, above your head, "up above your space."
4. **Ascend past the universe.** Imagine rising above the room, the sky, the planet — out past the universe.
5. **Layers of light.** Pass into a **big beautiful white light**; keep going up through **light after light** — many layers of bright light separated by thin bands of **dark light** ("just a layer before the next light — keep going").
6. **The golden light.** Pass through a great **golden light** — keep going.
7. **The Laws — jelly-like substance.** Enter a **jelly-like substance containing all the colors of the rainbow** that shifts colors as you move through it: this is the **Sixth Plane, the Laws**. Teaching notes: avoid drifting into the **deep blue light** (the Law of Magnetism, which can pull you in); a **pink mist** may appear (the Law of Compassion), which "pushes you onward."
8. **The pearlescent window.** Ahead is an **iridescent, pearly white-blue light**, rectangular "like a window." This window is the **opening to the Seventh Plane**. Go through it, deep inside it.
9. **Immersion — becoming All That Is.** Feel the whitish glow pass **through your body**; the sense of separation between body and energy dissolves — "you become part of All That Is." Reassurances taught: your body won't disappear; it "becomes perfect and healthy." **There are only energies here, not people or beings — "if you see people, go higher."**
10. **Command/Request.** From this connected state, make the command: *"Creator of All That Is, it is commanded (requested) that [healing/reading/belief change] be done in the highest and best way. Thank you. It is done, it is done, it is done."*
11. **Witness.** Move your consciousness into the client's space (through their crown, with permission) and **watch until the Creator's work is finished** — the healing counts only when witnessed to completion.
12. **Return.** In the classic (early) version: rinse yourself off in white light, return your consciousness down through your crown, send energy back down to the Earth's center, ground, and make an "energy break." In the **updated Seventh-Plane teaching**, Stibal dropped the ritual return: since you *are* part of All That Is, you never actually "leave," so no energy break or grounding-out is required — you simply open your eyes, staying connected. (Practitioner materials differ on this point depending on vintage; the current official teaching is the "stay connected" version.)
13. **The shortcut.** Experienced practitioners are taught the road map eventually collapses into an instantaneous act of intention — "just go up" — since the Seventh Plane is "everywhere and within."

## 5. Key Vocabulary / Glossary

- **Going up / out of your space** — the crown-to-cosmos visualization; entering theta and connecting to the Creator.
- **Command / Request** — the operative verbal formula; "command" disciplines the practitioner's subconscious, "request" petitions the Creator. Always closed with *"Thank you. It is done, it is done, it is done."*
- **Witnessing** — mentally observing the Creator's energy completing a healing/change inside the client's body-space; mandatory for the work to "count."
- **Readings** — intuitive sessions: entering a client's space (verbal permission required) through the crown and perceiving information. Includes the **body scan** — a gentle intuitive sweep of the client's body ("go in as gently as a feather") to "see" organs, emotions, entities, and stored trauma.
- **Belief work** — the flagship technique: locating and replacing subconscious "programs" (belief statements that test as "true" in the body) by commanding the Creator to **pull, cancel/resolve, and replace** them.
- **Four levels of belief:**
  - **Core** — learned/accepted in this life from childhood; "held as energy in the frontal lobe."
  - **Genetic** — inherited from ancestors (7+ generations) or added to genes this life; "held in the morphogenetic field around the master cell in the pineal gland."
  - **History** — past lives, deep genetic memory, collective consciousness; held in the aura; these are "resolved" (not pulled) to keep their lessons.
  - **Soul** — the deepest level, "all that a person is," pulled "off the completeness" beginning at the heart chakra outward.
- **Program** — a belief lodged at one or more levels that behaves like software governing life and health.
- **Digging (digging work)** — the structured questioning technique (e.g., "and what's the worst thing about that?", "who taught you that?", "what would happen if…?") to drill from surface complaints down to the **bottom/key belief**; changing the keystone belief collapses the whole stack. Codified in *Digging for Beliefs* (2019).
- **Muscle testing / energy testing** — kinesiology-style verification: baseline the client on "yes/no" (finger-ring resistance or standing sway), then have them state a belief aloud ("I hate my father") to test whether the program is held; used before and after belief work on all four levels.
- **Downloads** — positive feelings/knowings transmitted "from the Creator" into the client (with verbal per-item consent): e.g., "Would you like to know what it feels like to live without fear? To be loved?" Rationale: many people have never *felt* the target state, and the subconscious accepts what "comes from a divine place." Seminars involve hundreds of rapid-fire downloads.
- **Feeling work** — the umbrella for teaching the body/subconscious *feelings* (via downloads) as opposed to changing *beliefs* (belief work); the two are paired.
- **Gene work / DNA activation** — from the Basic DNA seminar: commanding activation of the "youth and vitality chromosome" and the 12-strand DNA (activating "shadow strands" and telomeres), performed on the "master cell" in the pineal gland.
- **Manifesting** — theta-state declaration of desired outcomes "in the highest and best way," combined with digging out subconscious blocks; doctrine holds you manifest constantly, so beliefs must be cleaned first (*Manifesting & Abundance* seminar).
- **Future readings** — viewing a client's **most probable future**, explicitly framed as non-fixed: futures shift when beliefs change; used motivationally and diagnostically.
- **Guardian angels** — protective beings (often Fourth-Plane ancestors or Fifth-Plane angels) assigned to individuals; practitioners are taught to "meet" a client's guardian angels in readings.
- **Waywards** — earthbound spirits stuck between the Third and Fourth Planes ("missed their window to the next evolvement"); may attach to people/places; the practitioner commands they be **"sent to the Creator's light."**
- **The Seventh Plane / All That Is** — see Section 3; also shorthand for the correct "place" to work from.
- **Oaths, vows, obligations / energy exchanges** — binding commitments (this life or "past lives") to planes 1–6 or their beings; found via digging and revoked by command ("released, resolved, and done").
- **Free agency** — the inviolable free-will rule; underpins the verbal-permission requirement.
- **Creator's perspective / "Creator, what do I need to know?"** — the standing instruction to ask the Creator rather than interpret alone; answers arrive as visions/knowings.
- **Psychic senses** — clairvoyance, clairaudience, clairsentience, empathy, prophecy — mapped to chakras and trained in seminars.
- **Intuitive anatomy** — the 15-day advanced study of organs/systems and the emotions-beliefs allegedly stored in each.
- **Heart song, soul fragments, curses, implants, "Baby in the womb," "Heal the broken soul"** — Advanced-seminar cleanup techniques (retrieving scattered "soul fragments," breaking curses, releasing "psychic hooks," healing gestational/birth trauma).

## 7. Self-Framing vs. External Description (brief)

ThetaHealing frames itself as "a meditation training technique and spiritual philosophy — not a religion," compatible with all faiths, which "always recommends" clients continue conventional medical care, and which merely teaches a focused-prayer technique whose results depend on the Creator and the client's beliefs. External observers describe it very differently: Wikipedia and RationalWiki class it as an **esoteric pseudoscience** with no credible evidence of efficacy; McGill's Office for Science and Society documents Stibal publicly claiming ThetaHealing could regrow an amputated leg and an ovary; the UK press and Advertising Standards Authority have acted against practitioners' cancer-cure claims; and in *Robinson v. Stibal* (Idaho Supreme Court, 2016) a jury heard expert testimony that Stibal's records show she was **never definitively diagnosed with cancer**, undermining the founding narrative.

## 8. Source Notes and Disagreements

- **Founding diagnosis:** official story (bone cancer, instant healing) vs. court-record account (no definitive diagnosis; slow healing). Both versions circulate; the official curriculum teaches only the former.
- **Plane numbering:** canonical order (1 = minerals → 7 = Creator) vs. occasional inverted summaries in secondary sources. Trust the books/official seminar pages.
- **Scale claims:** "25+ countries" (older bios) vs. "180+ countries, hundreds of thousands of practitioners" (current marketing); no independent audit of either.
- **Grounding/energy break:** early books teach returning/grounding after each session; current "Seventh Plane" teaching says no return is needed. Practitioner materials differ by vintage.
- **Waywards' location:** most sources place them "between the Third and Fourth Planes"; a few practitioner sites describe them loosely as "Fourth-Plane spirits."
- **HQ:** older references say Idaho Falls/Ammon, Idaho; current headquarters is Bigfork, Montana.
