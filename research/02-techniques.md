# ThetaHealing (Vianna Stibal): Techniques & Methodology — Expert Knowledge-Base Report

> **Scope note.** This report documents the *claimed* techniques and internal methodology of ThetaHealing as taught by Vianna Stibal (founder, 1995, Idaho Falls) and her certified instructors, compiled from public web sources, seminar descriptions, practitioner sites, and Stibal's published books (*ThetaHealing*, *Advanced ThetaHealing*, *ThetaHealing: Digging for Beliefs*, *Seven Planes of Existence*). It is descriptive, not an endorsement: ThetaHealing is classified as an esoteric/energy-healing pseudoscience with no scientific validation; muscle testing rests on the ideomotor effect (see §2.6). Passages marked "reconstructed" are synthesized from teaching materials rather than quoted from a single transcript.

## 0. Foundational Framework (needed to understand every technique)

- **Theta state claim:** All techniques are performed while the practitioner holds a theta brainwave state (4–7 Hz), induced in seconds via a guided visualization called the **Road Map to the Seventh Plane**. Stibal claims EEG measurement shows energy concentrating at the crown during the process.
- **Road Map / going up (the universal entry ritual for every technique):**
  1. Sit, feet flat, hands relaxed; take a deep breath. Ground and center in the heart space.
  2. Visualize energy/consciousness going **down through the feet into the center of Mother Earth** ("part of All That Is").
  3. Draw that energy **up through the soles, through the body and all seven chakras**.
  4. Exit **through the crown chakra in a "beautiful ball of light."**
  5. Travel up past the planes of existence: past a bright white light, a dark light, another bright light, a golden light, a thick "jello-like" layer (the Laws), into an iridescent, **pearlescent/opalescent white light — the Seventh Plane**, the "energy of creation" / Creator of All That Is.
  6. There, **make the command** (see §4), then move consciousness into the client's space to witness the work.
  - Later teaching (Advanced/Seven Planes era) adds a shortcut: "expand into All That Is" — realizing you are already part of Seventh-Plane energy rather than traveling.
- **The Seven Planes of Existence (philosophical map):** 1st — minerals, rocks, earth; 2nd — plants, vitamins; 3rd — humans/animals (the plane of ego, beliefs, drama); 4th — spirits/ancestors; 5th — angels, ascended masters, Councils of Twelve (dual: high/low); 6th — the Laws (Law of Magnetism, of Time, etc.) and pure tones/virtues; 7th — Creator of All That Is, pure creative energy of unconditional love. Doctrine: healing from planes 1–6 involves "trades, oaths and obligations"; only Seventh-Plane work is said to be instant and free of energetic cost.
- **"Command" vs. "request":** The operative phrase is "**Creator of All That Is, it is commanded (or requested) that…**". "Command" is taught as addressed to *one's own subconscious* (to bypass doubt), not as ordering God; those uncomfortable say "requested." Every command ends "**Thank you. It is done. It is done. It is done.**" — "Thank you" asserts completed co-creation; the triple "it is done" is taught as sealing it on (variously) mind/body/spirit or the three levels of acceptance, and shifting the practitioner into pure witnessing.
- **Witnessing doctrine:** "The Creator does the healing; the practitioner witnesses it." Nothing is considered to have happened unless the practitioner *observes it as visualization to completion* in the client's space. If the practitioner cannot "see" it finish, the work is considered incomplete.
- **Free will / consent doctrine:** No healing, belief change, or download without the client's **verbal permission**, item by item. This is a formal certification requirement (see §6.4).

## 1. Belief Work

### 1.1 The premise
"Programs" are beliefs held as energy that the subconscious treats as operating instructions; negative programs are claimed to create illness, blocks, and repeating drama. Belief work = locate the program (muscle test), get permission, and have the Creator "pull, cancel, resolve and replace" it on all levels where it exists.

### 1.2 The four levels of belief
| Level | Content | Claimed storage location | Removal/replacement rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core** | Beliefs taught/accepted in this life from childhood ("what we are taught and accept in our childhoods") | Energy in the **frontal lobe** of the brain / neurons | Pulled and released to God's light; replaced with a new program from the Creator |
| **Genetic (ancestral)** | Programs inherited from ancestors or added to genes in this life; taught as reaching **7 generations back (and forward)** | The **morphogenetic field** around the physical DNA (in the pituitary "master cell" model) | Pulled from the morphogenetic field, sent to God's light, replaced |
| **History** | Anything past 7 generations: past-life memories, deep genetic memory, land memories, collective-consciousness experiences | The auric field / energy around head and shoulders | **Not simply pulled — must be RESOLVED** (the lesson extracted, energies of the memory resolved) then sent to God's light; free-floating memories cancelled. Doctrine: if you delete without resolving/learning the lesson, the program "shifts back" |
| **Soul** | "All that we are" — beliefs so deep they permeate the whole being | Pulled off the **complete being, beginning at the heart chakra and moving outward** | Pulled, cancelled, replaced like core but from the whole essence |

**Key operating rule:** a program can sit on one, several, or all four levels simultaneously. If removed from one level but left on another, it re-installs itself. Hence practitioners test and clear **on all four levels** ("4-level belief work"), and the standard command names all four.

### 1.3 How beliefs are identified
1. **Client interview** — opening question in Stibal's canon: *"If you could change any one thing in your life, what would you change first?"*
2. **Intuitive reading / body scan** (see §5.7) — practitioner claims to "see" blocks.
3. **Muscle testing (energy testing)** — the client states the suspected program in the first person, present tense ("I am afraid of success," "I have to be sick to get love"); a strong/"yes" response = program present; weak/"no" = absent (§2). Testing is also used *after* work to verify removal.
4. **Belief questionnaires** — e.g., the Manifesting & Abundance course's **100-belief abundance questionnaire** the student muscle-tests through to map blocks.
5. **Dual/paired testing** — testing both a belief and its opposite ("I am worthy" / "I am unworthy") to detect **dual beliefs** (both testing yes), which signal programs on different levels.

### 1.4 Digging — the flagship discovery technique
Digging is "compassionate inquiry" to descend from the **surface belief** through stacked layers to the **bottom (root/key) belief** — the one holding the whole stack. Doctrine: pull only the bottom belief and the stack above it collapses ("hundreds of beliefs can shift at once"); pull only surface beliefs and the issue regrows.

**Generic digging procedure (Basic-DNA canon):**
1. Identify the presenting issue; muscle-test a first statement about it.
2. Ask open laddering questions: **who, what, where, when, why, how** — "When did this start?", "Who taught you this?", "How does this serve you?", "What are you learning from it?"
3. The central ladder is the **"what would happen if…" chain**: *"What would happen if you [changed / succeeded / were healthy / let it go]?" → client answers → "And what would be the worst thing about that?" / "And then what would happen?"* — repeated, each answer restated as a first-person belief and (optionally) muscle-tested.
4. **Signs the bottom belief is reached:** a strong emotional/energetic shift (tears, laughter, "aha," visible relief); the client's answers loop back on themselves; the practitioner muscle-tests "we have reached the bottom belief" / tests the candidate program and its feeder programs; frequently the bottom belief is startlingly literal ("If I get well, I will die," "I am safe only if I am poor").
5. Get verbal permission, then run the pull/replace command (§1.6) on the bottom belief; **re-test the whole ladder** — associated beliefs should now test "no" without individual pulling; pull any stragglers; finish with supporting downloads (§3).

**The digging shortcuts / approaches (Dig Deeper course & *Digging for Beliefs* book — the "8 ways of digging," some sources count 10):** each is a question-track chosen by the presenting flavor of the issue:
1. **Fear digging** — chase the fear to its terminal image: "What is the worst that could happen if…?" repeated to the end of the chain (the bottom is usually a death/annihilation/aloneness program). Key discriminator taught: *"Is it a fear of failure, or actually a fear of success?"*
2. **Resentment digging** — for anger/resentment toward a person: "What did they do? What would it mean to forgive them? **How does holding this serve you?**" Bottom beliefs are typically secondary-gain programs; resolution includes forgiveness work and releasing the resentment (the 3 Rs — resentment, regret, rejection — are a dedicated Advanced exercise, §5.9).
3. **Grief/sadness digging** — around loss: what remains unsaid/unfinished; what the client believes they must hold to stay connected to the deceased.
4. **Anger digging** — anger as a cover emotion; dig for what is underneath (usually fear or hurt).
5. **Illness/survival digging** — "What would you have to change in your life to be completely well?" "What is the illness doing *for* you?" "Who would you be without it?" (secondary-gain track: illness as protector, rest-permission, love-getter).
6. **Manifesting/blocks digging** — for goals that won't materialize: "What would happen if you had it?" (uncovers safety/deserving programs, oaths of poverty §5.4).
7. **"Impossible" digging** — when the client says something is impossible: dig on the impossibility itself.
8. **Genetic & history digging** — when muscle testing locates a program on the genetic/history level with no this-life origin: dig into ancestral patterns ("Does this feel like yours or your family's?") or past-life/collective material, resolve the lesson, release vows made in that context.

**Practitioner craft rules taught:** never lead the witness (ask, don't tell); keep the client talking in their own words; write the ladder down; stay in theta and "ask the Creator" what question to ask next; hold compassion — "listen beyond the words"; be specific with commands; don't stop at the first emotional belief (it may be a **key belief** but not the bottom); use muscle testing to verify the bottom has been reached.

### 1.5 Feeling work vs. belief work (doctrinal distinction)
- **Belief work** removes/replaces *programs* (changes behavior and life-patterns).
- **Feeling work / downloads** (§3) *teaches* the nervous system feelings it has allegedly never experienced (joy, safety, being loved), instilled "from the Creator's perspective in seconds" rather than learned over years. Doctrine: some clients cannot hold a new belief because they lack the feeling it requires — so digging sessions interleave both: pull the program, then download the missing feelings.

### 1.6 How beliefs are "pulled, cancelled, resolved and replaced" — the command
Canonical wording (public practitioner sources):

> *"Creator of All That Is, it is commanded (or requested) that the program/belief of '[belief stated exactly]' be **pulled on all four levels — core, genetic, history and soul — cancelled, resolved on the history level, and sent to God's light** from [client's name], and **replaced with** '[new program — 'whatever the Creator says' or the agreed positive belief]'. Thank you. It is done. It is done. It is done."*

Procedure around the command: obtain verbal permission for both the pull **and** the specific replacement → go up (Road Map) → make the command → move into the client's space and **witness** the old energy leaving to "God's light" and the new program entering on each level (frontal lobe / DNA field / auric field / whole being) → return, ground → **re-muscle-test** the old belief (should now test "no") and the new one (should test "yes"). Replacement is mandatory doctrine — a pulled program must never leave a vacuum; if the client doesn't want a stated replacement, the practitioner asks the Creator for "the Creator's perspective/definition" to install.

## 2. Muscle Testing (Energy Testing)

### 2.1 Role
ThetaHealing's verification instrument: claimed readout of the *subconscious* (electromagnetic field strengthens on statements the subconscious holds true, weakens on false). Used before work (find programs), after work (verify change), and to check "bottom belief reached." Stated in first person, present tense, in the client's own language.

### 2.2 Hydration prerequisite
Always checked first. Dehydration is taught as the #1 cause of false/erratic responses (rationale given: electrolyte conductivity). Test: have the person say "yes"/"no" and see if responses differentiate; if mushy, drink water (some practitioners add a pinch of salt or have the client hold water) and retest. Clients are told to arrive hydrated.

### 2.3 Calibration of yes/no
Before any content testing:
1. State known-true fact: "My name is [real name]" → must test **strong/yes**.
2. State known-false: "My name is [wrong name]" → must test **weak/no**.
3. Common secondary pair: "I am a woman" / "I am a man."
4. Some teach explicitly *programming* the responses: say "'Yes' means my fingers hold / body tips forward; 'No' means fingers open / body tips back," repeat "yes, yes, yes" and "no, no, no" until the pattern is consistent.
If calibration fails: hydrate, ground, have the client rub the sternum/thymus, re-center, or switch methods.

### 2.4 Methods
- **Finger/hand method (practitioner-on-client):** client presses thumb and middle (or index) finger together into a ring on the dominant hand; client states the belief; practitioner hooks fingers/thumbs into the ring and applies steady, gentle pull ("like pliers"). Fingers hold = yes/true-in-subconscious; fingers part = no. Variant: client makes two interlocking finger-rings and tries to pull them apart themselves.
- **Self-testing finger loop:** same ring, tested by one's own other hand — used by practitioners for self-work and quick checks.
- **Standing/sway method:** stand feet hip-width, knees soft, eyes closed, ideally facing north (some teach this). Calibrate: "Yes, yes, yes" → body tips gently **forward**; "No, no, no" → tips **backward**. Then state each belief and read the sway. Preferred for clients who can't do finger tests and for remote/self work; also the method most visible to the client (a transparency/consent feature — the client feels their own body answer).

### 2.5 Rules of use
- Test *statements*, not questions; one program per test.
- Test the belief **and** its opposite (dual-belief detection).
- Retest after every pull/download.
- **Do not muscle-test future predictions** (explicit canon caution) — testing reads present programs, not the future.
- If results contradict the client's conscious view, that gap *is* the finding (conscious vs. subconscious mismatch).

### 2.6 Limitations (both internal and external)
- Internal (taught): dehydration; client resistance; tester pressing too hard/unevenly; asking ambiguous statements; practitioner "leading."
- External (scientific): muscle testing is an **ideomotor effect** — responses reflect expectation and unconscious cueing of practitioner and client; controlled studies find applied-kinesiology-style testing unreliable for diagnosis; the tester's agenda demonstrably biases outcomes. ThetaHealing sources themselves concede the practitioner can influence the test, which is why "clean, neutral" questioning is stressed.

## 3. Downloads

### 3.1 Definition
A "download" is the claimed instantaneous instilling of a feeling, knowing, or program **from the Creator of All That Is / Seventh Plane** into all four levels of the person — teaching in seconds what would otherwise take a lifetime to learn. Used to (a) install replacement programs after pulling, (b) supply never-felt feelings (feeling work), (c) close every session on a positive state.

### 3.2 Procedure
1. **State the download aloud** and obtain **verbal permission** ("yes") for *each* download or list (in classes, students consent to receive the manual's download lists in bulk).
2. Practitioner goes up (Road Map), commands: *"Creator of All That Is, it is commanded that the feeling/knowing of [X] be instilled in [name] on all four levels, in the highest and best way, from the Seventh Plane of Existence. Thank you. It is done, it is done, it is done."*
3. **Witness** the energy entering every cell/level; ground; optionally muscle-test that the download "took."

### 3.3 Canonical phrasings (the download grammar)
Downloads are phrased as *knowings*, each considered a distinct teaching:
- **"I know what it feels like to…"** (the feeling itself)
- **"I know how to…"** (the skill)
- **"I know when to…"** / "I know that I can…" (timing/possibility)
- **"I know how to live my daily life with/without…"** (integration)
- **"I know it is possible to…"**, **"I know it is safe to…"**, **"I am / I have…"** (identity form)
- **"I know the Creator's definition/perspective of…"** (used when human definitions are distorted — e.g., Creator's definition of love, of forgiveness)
A full download of one theme typically runs the whole battery, e.g. for safety: *I know what safety feels like; I know how to feel safe; I know it is possible to be safe; I know how to live my daily life feeling safe; I know the Creator's definition of safety…*

### 3.4 Common download list examples (public sources)
- *"I know what it feels like to be inspired."*
- *"I know what it feels like to speak my truth without fear of judgment."*
- *"I know what it feels like to live in harmony with all around me and all circumstances."*
- *"I know how to make quick, clear decisions."*
- Themes with published lists: communication, self-love, abundance/money, divine timing, releasing self-judgment, safety, worthiness, forgiveness. Course manuals (Basic, Advanced, Manifesting & Abundance) contain hundreds; Advanced DNA is largely structured around mass "downloads from the instructor."

### 3.5 Feeling work vs. belief work (operational)
Belief work = subtractive+substitutive (pull program, insert program). Feeling work = purely additive (no pull needed; the person has a *deficit*, not a block). Diagnostic: muscle-test *"I know what it feels like to be loved"* — a "no" indicates feeling work, not digging.

## 4. The Healing Technique (physical healing / "witnessing a healing")

### 4.1 Doctrinal core
The practitioner heals nothing; they **connect, command, and witness**. "When the healer connects to the Seventh Plane and witnesses the Creator's power to heal, the healing will be done." Unconditional-love energy is "grabbed" at the Seventh Plane and placed into the body.

### 4.2 Step-by-step protocol (Basic DNA canon, public reconstructions)
1. **Permission:** ask the client verbally: "Do I have your permission to do a healing on you?" (For absent third parties, practitioners are taught to ask the person's "higher self" and to accept refusal — a "no" is honored; healing without permission is forbidden.)
2. **Ground and go up:** Road Map to the Seventh Plane (§0).
3. **Make the command:** *"Creator of All That Is, it is commanded (requested) that an unconditional healing of [full name] occur on [condition/whole body] at this moment, in the highest and best way. Thank you. It is done. It is done. It is done."*
4. **Enter and witness:** move consciousness gently into the client's space/body ("as gently as a feather floating on the breeze"); go to the affected area; **watch** the Creator's energy perform the change until the process visibly completes. "Stay in the challenged area until the healing energy is finished." No visualization-of-completion → repeat/return later.
5. **Rinse and return:** "**Rinse yourself off** with Seventh-Plane energy/white light," pull consciousness out of the client's space, return awareness through the crown, **ground back** — send energy down into the earth, back up to the heart space; make an "**energy break**" (a taught gesture/intent that severs any lingering cord with the client's energy or issue).
6. **Debrief:** ask what the client felt; if beliefs are holding the illness (very common in doctrine), move to digging — physical healing is taught as usually *gated by* belief work ("the body won't hold the healing over a program that requires the illness").

### 4.3 Notes
- Healing is always framed as complementary; certified practitioners are instructed never to advise stopping medical care (breaches occur and are the modality's chief documented harm vector).
- "Group healings" and remote healings follow the same command/witness format.

## 5. Additional Techniques (module by module)

### 5.1 DNA Activation
Claim: activating the "12 strands" — the 2 physical strands plus 10 dormant "shadow strands" — and the **"youth and vitality chromosome"**, strengthening telomeres to slow/reverse aging and boost immunity. Procedure (public outline): ground; go up; command activation; move into the **Master Cell in the pineal gland** ("operation center of all cells; the beginning point of healing"); witness the Creator building the shadow strands from amino acids until the strands/chromosome pair is formed and 'plugged in'; witness the activation ripple to all cells; rinse, ground. Historically done once per person; taught on day one of Basic DNA (every student is activated).

### 5.2 Gene Work / Gene Replacement (DNA 2 / Advanced material)
Claimed repair or "replacement" of defective genes/programs in the DNA: with permission, go up, command that the defective gene/program be changed "to the Creator's perfect blueprint," witness the change in the Master Cell and its propagation to all cells. Also covers pulling *genetic beliefs* (7-generations doctrine) from the morphogenetic field around the DNA. (No scientific mechanism; documented here as taught.)

### 5.3 Manifesting (Manifesting & Abundance canon)
- **Doctrine:** you manifest what your *subconscious* holds; spoken affirmation ≈30–40% effective, visualization ≈50%, **manifesting inside a theta state ≈80–90%** (Stibal's claimed figures). Everything divine is requested "in the **highest and best way**" — the standard qualifier protecting against ill-formed wishes; requests are made from alignment, "not fear or lack."
- **Procedure:** clear the blocks first (dig the abundance questionnaire's ~100 beliefs; release **oaths of poverty** §5.4); then: go up to the Seventh Plane → command/state the manifestation — commonly taught **stated three times** — *"Creator, it is commanded that [X] or something better manifest in my life in the highest and best way. Thank you. It is done, it is done, it is done."* → **visualize/step into the future reality as already real** (feel it, see details, "remember your future") → release attachment to timing (**Divine Timing** downloads) → act in the world.
- Includes "creating space to manifest" (clearing clutter/energetic space) and "seeing/remembering your future" exercises.

### 5.4 Curses, Vows, Oaths, Obligations (Advanced)
- Category: soul-level bindings carried from this life, ancestors, or past lives ("history"): **vows of poverty, chastity, silence, suffering; oaths of loyalty; curses given/received; obligations and soul contracts; implants; psychic hooks**.
- Detection: muscle test ("I have an oath of poverty," "I am cursed," "I must be poor to be spiritual"); or intuitively during digging when money/relationship blocks won't clear.
- Release procedure: identify the origin event (digging into genetic/history level); **extract the lesson** ("What did you learn? Is it complete?"); with permission command: *"Creator, it is commanded that this vow/oath/curse of [X] be declared finished and complete, released on all four levels, cancelled, resolved and sent to God's light, and replaced with [Creator's program]. Thank you. It is done ×3."* Witness it leave; replace; retest. **Oath of poverty** is the flagship example (typically framed as a past-life monastic vow still running "money is unspiritual") — released as "fulfilled/complete," then replaced with abundance programs and downloads.

### 5.5 Soul Mate Work (Soul Mate seminar; book *Finding Your Soul Mate with ThetaHealing*)
- Doctrine: many compatible soul mates exist, not one; blocks (unworthiness, fear of engulfment, vows of chastity/celibacy, "all men/women are…" programs) keep them away.
- Method: dig to the **bottom belief blocking a compatible soul mate**; clear vows (celibacy!), heartbreak residue, and the 3 Rs; download self-love/worthiness; then the **calling exercise**: go up, command *"Creator, it is commanded that my **most compatible soul mate** be sent to me in the highest and best way [often: specifying developed qualities, and 'that I recognize them']. Thank you. It is done ×3,"* witness the "call" going out. Practitioners warn to specify "most compatible" (canonical cautionary tales about under-specified lists).

### 5.6 Future Readings
Practitioner goes up, commands to be shown the client's **probable** future on a question, witnesses/relays imagery. Canon cautions: the future is **not set in stone** — readings show the probable path given current beliefs; belief work changes the trajectory (that is the point: read the future, then dig/clear what creates the unwanted version). Explicit rule: **never muscle-test predictions**; avoid death-date or doom pronouncements.

### 5.7 Body Scans / Intuitive Anatomy (basics)
- **Body scan (Basic):** with permission, go up, command *"Creator, show me [name]'s body; show me what you see"*; move consciousness in through the crown "gently as a feather floating on the breeze" (forcing makes the body "resist and not show itself"); observe organs/systems as intuitive imagery (colors, dark spots, symbols); withdraw, rinse, ground, report neutrally, **never diagnose** (refer to physicians). Beginners are taught to verify impressions against known conditions to train accuracy.
- **Intuitive Anatomy (advanced course):** system-by-system tour (weeks-long) pairing each organ/system with its claimed emotional/belief themes (e.g., liver–anger, kidneys–fear, pancreas–sweetness/joy…), with digging and downloads per system.

### 5.8 Guardian Angel Readings
A Basic-DNA exercise: with client's permission, go up, command to **see/speak with the person's guardian angels**, witness them, relay messages; client may ask questions. Taught with Fifth-Plane cautions (spirits/angels are plane-5, dual — practitioners are told to go *through the Seventh Plane first* and command truth, rather than contacting entities directly, to avoid "trades" and deception).

### 5.9 Other named techniques worth indexing
- **The 3 Rs release** (Advanced): commanded release of **resentments, regrets, rejection** wholesale, with forgiveness work.
- **Free-floating memories** (Advanced): memories recorded during shock/anesthesia/unconsciousness are commanded "cancelled and sent to God's light" (taught as resolving post-surgical panic patterns).
- **Heal the Broken Soul** (Advanced): commanded mending of a "shattered" soul from catastrophic grief/trauma.
- **Baby in the Womb / Send Love to the Baby in the Womb** (Advanced): witnessing love sent to one's own fetal self, clearing womb-period programs.
- **Soul fragments** (Advanced): commanded retrieval/return of soul fragments exchanged in relationships/traumas, washed in Seventh-Plane light.
- **Seven Planes meditation**: expanding through all planes for philosophical grounding.
- **Waywards/implants clearing** (Advanced/DNA3 material): removal of claimed intrusive energies — through the Seventh Plane only.

## 6. Session Structure (1:1)

### 6.1 Typical flow (composite of official Help-Center + practitioner descriptions)
1. **Opening conversation (5–15 min):** issue intake; canonical opener *"If you could change any one thing in your life, what would you change first?"*; expectation-setting; consent framing ("nothing changes without your verbal permission"); hydration check + water offered.
2. **Attunement:** brief co-meditation; practitioner runs the Road Map, connects "to the Creator," goes into theta (client just relaxes, eyes open or closed — no client skill needed).
3. **Optional body scan / intuitive reading** relayed to client.
4. **Muscle-test calibration** (yes/no, hydration), then testing of candidate beliefs.
5. **Digging block (the bulk, 20–40 min):** conversational laddering to bottom belief; periodic muscle tests.
6. **Clearing block:** per bottom belief — verbal permission → pull/cancel/resolve/replace command → witness → retest ladder.
7. **Downloads block:** feeling downloads read aloud item-by-item, each with "yes" consent; optional witnessed physical healing (with permission).
8. **Closing:** grounding back (practitioner and client), energy break/rinse; recap of what shifted; retests demonstrated to client; integration advice (water, rest, journaling, possible "healing detox"/vivid dreams); next-session planning.

### 6.2 Logistics
- **Duration:** ~60 minutes standard (practitioners run 60–90; first sessions sometimes 90). Remote (phone/video) sessions are considered fully equivalent — the work is claimed non-local.
- **Cadence:** single-issue resolution is claimed possible in one session; chronic/layered issues 3–10 sessions.
- **Client state:** awake, conversational throughout (unlike hypnosis); client does not need to enter theta.

### 6.3 Practitioner checklist (as taught)
- Hydrate self and client; ground; clear own beliefs about the client's issue first ("clear yourself before session").
- Go up before *every* command; command specifically; witness *to completion*; always replace what is pulled; always resolve on history level; rinse + energy break after entering anyone's space; retest everything; keep session notes of the belief ladder; stay in "Creator's perspective," not sympathy.
- Never: work without verbal permission; muscle-test the future; diagnose or countermand medical care; install a belief the client hasn't approved verbatim.

### 6.4 Ethics of consent
Consent is the modality's most emphasized rule: certified practitioners "cannot even give you a positive belief until you give verbal permission"; each download and each belief replacement is spoken aloud and okayed; free will is framed as absolute. For healings on third parties/absent persons, permission is sought from the person or (doctrinally) their "higher self," and a refusal terminates the attempt. ThetaHealing certification binds practitioners to an ethics code (legal name use of trademark, scope limits, no medical claims) — enforcement quality varies and is a standing criticism.

## 7. Worked Example: Full Digging Dialogue

*(Reconstructed from teaching materials and published fragments — the "mother whose child is leaving for university" scenario used publicly to teach fear-digging, expanded to a full session arc in canonical format. Not a verbatim transcript.)*

**P = Practitioner, C = Client.** Presenting issue: anxiety about daughter leaving home.

- **P:** If you could change one thing in your life right now, what would it be first?
- **C:** This constant dread about my daughter moving out for university.
- **P:** Let's test it. Say: "I am afraid of my daughter leaving home." *(Muscle test → strong/yes.)* Your body says yes. What would be **the worst thing that could happen** if she leaves?
- **C:** I'd be… left on my own.
- **P:** Say: "If my daughter leaves, I will be all alone." *(→ yes.)* And **what would be the worst thing about** being left on your own?
- **C:** No one would need me anymore.
- **P:** "I am only valuable when I am needed." *(→ yes.)* If no one needed you — **then what would happen?**
- **C:** I don't know… I guess I wouldn't matter. I'd just disappear.
- **P:** Stay with that. **What happens to someone who doesn't matter?**
- **C:** *(long pause, eyes wet)* They get left. People leave them and they die alone.
- **P:** Say: "If I am not needed, I will be abandoned and die alone." *(→ strong yes; tears — energy shift noted.)* When is the **first time** you felt that?

## Appendix: Critical-context one-liners for the knowledge base
- No mechanism or clinical evidence supports theta-state remote healing, DNA activation, or belief "pulling"; Stibal's own healing origin story (1995 "instant" leg healing) is uncorroborated.
- Muscle testing = ideomotor phenomenon; peer-reviewed work finds it unreliable and practitioner-biased.
- Main documented harms: medical avoidance/delay, costly course ladders, and grandiose claims; the internal consent ethic mitigates interpersonal but not epistemic risk.
- Strongest genuinely functional ingredients (from a secular view): structured Socratic laddering (digging ≈ downward-arrow technique from CBT), affirmation rehearsal, relaxation state, expectancy/placebo, and a warm therapeutic ritual with explicit consent.
