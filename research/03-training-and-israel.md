# ThetaHealing® Training System & the Israeli Scene — Research Report

*Compiled August 2026 from web research (thetahealing.com ecosystem pages, THInK, instructor sites, Israeli school sites). Note: ThetaHealing is an energy-healing / belief-work modality with no scientific evidence of medical efficacy; this report describes the system as its official materials present it.*

## 1. The Official Course Ladder

The system is a trademarked seminar pyramid owned by Vianna Stibal (founder, 1995, Idaho; now based in Bigfork, Montana). Completing **any practitioner seminar = certification in that seminar**; certificates are issued through THInK / thetahealing.com after the instructor registers the student. Each seminar includes an official manual (and often one of Vianna's books).

### 1.3 Instructor Track

- **Entry requirement:** Practitioner certification in **Basic DNA, Advanced DNA, Dig Deeper, You and the Creator** → eligible for the first Instructor seminar.
- **Instructor seminars are taught ONLY by Vianna Stibal and certified members of her family/team**, historically almost exclusively at **THInK in Bigfork, Montana** (with some world tour events; booking via ThetaHealingEvents.com).
- Each course has its own Instructor version. To teach seminar X you must hold Practitioner X + Instructor X.
- You can only teach the seminars for which you hold instructor certification, must follow official curricula/manuals, register students with HQ (per-student certification fees), and observe **minimum advertised pricing**.
- **Instructor certification lasts 4 years**; recertify by attending an Instructor seminar (as participant or assistant). Lapsed instructors may no longer teach but may keep practicing personally.
- Example THInK pricing seen: an Instructors-track seminar with Vianna at **$440 + $170 registration = $610** (short course); longer instructor certifications run higher; June + August are the big Montana instructor-season months.

## 2. Certifications, THInK & the Official Ecosystem

**Certification levels (thetahealing.com/certifications):**
1. **Certified ThetaHealer® / Practitioner** — granted per-seminar starting with Basic DNA. All practitioners sign a **contract** (trademark/usage rules). Practitioner certificates **don't expire**; there's no annual renewal fee to remain certified (paid directory listing/membership is optional).
2. **Certified Instructor** — per-seminar, via Vianna/THInK only; 4-year recertification cycle.
3. **ThetaHealing Master** — completion of practitioner seminars: Basic, Advanced, Dig Deeper, You and the Creator, Manifesting & Abundance, Intuitive Anatomy, World Relations, Disease & Disorder **plus** instructor seminars: Basic, Advanced, Dig Deeper, You and the Creator, M&A, IA, Intuitive Child in Me (~17 seminars total).
4. **Certificate of Science ("ThCS")** — Master requirements **+ DNA 3**. The top public credential.

**THInK (ThetaHealing Institute of Knowledge):** Vianna's headquarters school, 29048 Broken Leg Rd, Bigfork, MT 59911. Runs Vianna's practitioner and instructor seminars, issues certifications, hosts the summer "seminar season."

**Website ecosystem:**
- **thetahealing.com** — main site: seminar catalog, worldwide class search, **"Find a ThetaHealer" directory** (searchable by country/language/certification; claims practitioners in 180+ countries; ~600,000 practitioners claimed worldwide), practitioner dashboard, certifications pages, blog + **"Daily Meditations and Downloads with Vianna"** page, ThetaHealing Magazine.
- **thetahealinginstituteofknowledge.com** — THInK event calendar.
- **thetahealingevents.com** — instructor-seminar booking.
- **shop.thetahealing.com** — books, manuals, meditation audio, merchandise.
- **help.thetahealing.com** — Intercom help center (FAQs on certification, pricing/professionalism, session expectations).
- Localized official portals exist (e.g., japan.thetahealing.com, **thetahealingisrael.com** — a Hebrew-facing mirror of the seminar system).

**Vianna's seminars:** she personally teaches instructor certifications, DNA 3, DNA 4, Planes of Existence and "Enhancement Seminars" (with son Joshua and family), mainly Montana June–August, plus occasional international events.

## 4. Typical Costs Globally

- **Sessions:** no fixed price (official policy: each practitioner sets rates). Observed range: **$75–150/hr USD typical** (UK £30–200; 30–60 min sessions; package discounts common).
- **Basic DNA seminar:** official guidance ~**$400–500 USD** (minimum advertised pricing); real listings $400–675. Advanced DNA similar; Dig Deeper slightly less.
- **Intuitive Anatomy:** typically **$2,000–4,000 USD** globally (15 days).
- **Instructor seminars (Montana):** hundreds to a few thousand USD each; becoming a Master implies **tens of thousands of dollars** cumulative (17+ seminars, multiple Montana trips) — a frequent point of outside criticism (Wikipedia notes the MLM-like course pyramid and lack of scientific support).
- Registration/deposit fees ($100–170) on top of tuition are standard; repeat audits usually ~50% off or a flat repeat rate.

## 5. Daily / Weekly Personal Practice Between Courses

What official materials (Vianna's books, thetahealing.com, instructors' guidance) recommend:

1. **Daily morning theta meditation** — the "road map" ascent to the **7th Plane** every morning (the Basic DNA meditation): cleanse the energy field, fill with unconditional love, connect to Creator, "command" the best day, activate chakras. Official line: *"Each time you practice you go deeper into theta — you are training your brain."* The official site hosts **"Daily Meditations and Downloads with Vianna"**; Insight Timer / YouTube host sanctioned morning-meditation recordings with downloads.
2. **A minute of connection, morning and evening** — Vianna's minimal prescription: fill the body with unconditional love and hold **gratitude** daily.
3. **Daily downloads** — self-administering feeling downloads, especially the Advanced DNA download lists; many instructors send graduates daily/weekly download lists (WhatsApp groups are common in Israel — קבוצות ווטסאפ של בוגרים).
4. **Self-digging / belief journal** — regularly energy-test yourself (בוחן שריר), track limiting beliefs uncovered, dig to the root belief, replace and re-test; keep a written record of programs changed (a "digging journal" habit taught in Dig Deeper; the M&A questionnaire is reused as periodic self-audit).
5. **Manifesting routine** — the "Manifesting from the 7th Plane" meditation: state manifestations in theta, work Divine Timing, remove blocks when a manifestation stalls.
6. **Practice readings/scans and trade sessions** — official encouragement to "practice scanning and reading everything around you" and to exchange sessions with fellow practitioners between seminars (Israeli schools run מפגשי תרגול/practice evenings for alumni).
7. **Repeat seminars** cheaply (audit pricing) to refresh — explicitly encouraged by the pricing structure.
