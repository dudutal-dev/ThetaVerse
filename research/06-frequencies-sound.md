# Healing Frequencies & Sound — Expert Knowledge Base
### Research dossier for a ThetaHealing / meditation daily-practice app (Hebrew audience — תדרים מרפאים, ריפוי בצליל)

This report covers both **the practice world as practitioners teach it** (faithfully described, clearly labeled as tradition/claim) and **the scientific evidence** (honestly graded). Evidence grades used throughout: **A** = multiple RCTs/meta-analyses; **B** = some controlled trials, mixed; **C** = pilot/observational/animal only; **D** = no credible evidence / claim contradicted by known facts.

## 1. Brainwave Entrainment (היקשרות גלי מוח)

### 1.1 The three technologies

| Technology | Acoustic mechanism | Headphones? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Binaural beats** | Two pure carrier tones, one per ear (e.g., 200 Hz left + 206 Hz right). The beat (6 Hz) does not exist in the air — it is *constructed neurally* in the superior olivary complex of the brainstem from interaural phase differences. | **Required** (stereo, channel separation) | Beat is perceived only when carriers are below ~1,000–1,500 Hz; best perception at carriers ~**400–500 Hz**; frequency difference must be under ~30–35 Hz or the ear hears two separate tones. Perceived beat is faint — always mixed under music/noise in practice. ([bioRxiv auditory-pathway review](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/623231v1.full), [Brain.fm comparison](https://www.brain.fm/blog/binaural-beats-vs-monaural-beats-vs-isochronic-tones)) |
| **Monaural beats** | The two tones are mixed *before* reaching the ear; a real physical amplitude beat strikes the eardrum. | No — works on speakers | Stronger cortical (auditory steady-state) response than binaural in several EEG studies, because the beat is physically present. |
| **Isochronic tones** | A single tone switched on/off (amplitude-pulsed, sharp square/trapezoid envelope) at the target rate, e.g., a 528 Hz tone pulsed 6×/second. | No — works on speakers | Produces the strongest auditory steady-state response of the three; but **very little clinical research** exists specifically on isochronic tones ([Wikipedia: Isochronic tones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isochronic_tones), [literature review, Scielo](https://www.scielo.org.mx/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S1665-50442021000600238)). Practically useful for app audio that must work without headphones. |

### 1.2 Frequency targets (the standard band map)

| Band | Hz | Associated state (as taught) | Common app choices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delta | 0.5–4 | Deep dreamless sleep, healing/regeneration | 1–3 Hz for sleep tracks; one 2024 study even used a 0.25 Hz beat to shorten latency to slow-wave sleep in naps ([Sci Reports](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-76059-9)) |
| **Theta** | **4–8** | **The ThetaHealing state**: deep meditation, hypnagogia, memory/emotion access, "the state Vianna says the healer works from" | **4.5 Hz** (shamanic-drumming rate), **6 Hz** (best-studied theta beat), **7.83 Hz** (Schumann; marketed as "earth-tuned theta") |
| Alpha | 8–12 | Relaxed wakefulness, eyes-closed calm | 10 Hz for "calm focus," anxiety wind-down |
| Beta | 13–30 | Alert cognition | 14–18 Hz "focus" tracks |
| Gamma | 30–100 | Binding, insight; 40 Hz for the Alzheimer's research (see §1.5) | 40 Hz |

### 1.3 What the evidence actually shows (2023+)

- **Does entrainment (EEG change) actually happen?** The key modern reference is **Ingendoh, Posny & Heine 2023, PLOS ONE** — systematic review of 14 EEG studies: **5 supported entrainment, 8 contradicted it, 1 mixed**. Verdict: "inconclusive"; huge heterogeneity in beat frequency, carrier, exposure time, and EEG analysis ([PLOS ONE 2023](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0286023)). So the *mechanistic story is not established*, even where mood effects exist.
- **Anxiety/mood outcomes are more positive than the EEG story**: a 2024 systematic review of RCTs on personal, non-clinical binaural-beat use for stress management found some supportive trials but limited quality ([Tandfonline 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/18387357.2024.2374759)); a 2025 meta-analysis found benefit for **perioperative anxiety and pain** ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S096522992500175X)); a Cambridge systematic review in young adults found **small-to-moderate effects** on anxiety, sleep, and stress physiology ([Acta Neuropsychiatrica](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/acta-neuropsychiatrica/article/music-and-binaural-beat-interventions-for-young-adults-a-systematic-review-of-effects-on-anxiety-sleep-and-cognition/4282E1152FD3EACA5DF8304935165202)). The most replicated single finding across decades: **binaural beats reduce pre-operative/procedural anxiety**.
- **Honest framing for the app**: "May help many people relax and fall asleep; the relaxation effect is better supported than the 'brainwave synchronization' explanation." Effects may be partly expectation + masking sound + structured listening ritual — which still works for users.
- **Sleep (delta/theta)**: pilot-level positive results — delta binaural pilot improved sleep and post-sleep mood ([SAGE 2022 pilot](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/20552076221102243)); customized programs reduced Insomnia Severity Index in small trials; a Frontiers protocol for an all-night entrainment program exists ([Frontiers in Neurology](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neurology/articles/10.3389/fneur.2023.1024726/full)). Grade **B-/C**.

### 1.4 Theta-specific findings (the app's core band)

- **6 Hz binaural beat on a 250 Hz carrier, 30 min** → induced meditation-like theta increases; theta appeared across cortex **within ~10 min** (no significant change in the first 3 minutes) — Jirakittayakorn & Wongsawat 2017 ([Frontiers in Neuroscience](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2017.00365/full)).
- Daily 6 Hz listening for a month: ERP changes reported ([Sci Reports 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-68628-9)); brief self-administered theta sessions enhanced meditative/introspective state ratings ([PLOS ONE 2025](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0335580)).
- **Practical protocol synthesis** (what the literature + industry converge on):
 - **Carrier**: 200–400 Hz pure tones (research favorites: 250 Hz); keep carrier <500 Hz for best beat perception.
 - **Beat**: 4.5–7 Hz for meditation; 6 Hz best-studied; ramp designs (start alpha 10 Hz → descend to 6 Hz over 5–10 min) are industry standard "brainwave ladders."
 - **Session length**: minimum ~10 min to see EEG effects; **15–30 min is the sweet spot**; sleep tracks 30–60+ min.
 - **Volume**: low-moderate — entrainment stimuli do NOT need to be loud; beat layer typically mixed 15–25 dB under the music bed. ([Mind Machines guide](https://www.mindmachines.com/how-long-should-you-listen-to-binaural-beats-for-maximum-effect/), [BinauralPure protocol](https://binauralpure.com/blog/binaural-beats-for-meditation))
 - **Headphones required** for binaural; use isochronic/monaural for speaker playback (e.g., bedtime without earbuds).

### 1.5 Gamma 40 Hz (worth knowing, adjacent)

MIT's GENUS program (40 Hz light+sound) shows slowed brain atrophy and some cognitive preservation in mild Alzheimer's in Phase II; Phase III (Cognito Therapeutics) ongoing ([Picower Institute](https://picower.mit.edu/news/review-evidence-expanding-40hz-gamma-stimulation-promotes-brain-health), [MIT News 2025](https://news.mit.edu/2025/study-suggests-40hz-sensory-stimulation-may-benefit-some-alzheimers-patients-1114), [Frontiers review 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/aging-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnagi.2025.1710041/full)). This is the *one* frequency-specific stimulation line with serious clinical money behind it — but it is flicker+click sensory stimulation, not wellness audio; don't imply your app treats dementia.

## 2. Schumann Resonance — 7.83 Hz (תהודת שומאן)

**What it physically is**: electromagnetic standing waves in the Earth–ionosphere cavity, excited by ~50 lightning flashes/second worldwide; predicted by W.O. Schumann (1952), measured since the early 1960s. Fundamental ≈ 7.83 Hz with harmonics ~14.3, 20.8, 27.3, 33.8 Hz. The field is **electromagnetic, not acoustic**, and vanishingly weak (picotesla — far below the fields of household wiring). ([Daybreak Wire explainer](https://daybreakwire.com/science/schumann-resonance-explained/), [Wikipedia: Schumann resonances conspiracy theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schumann_resonances_conspiracy_theories))

**Why the wellness world loves it**: 7.83 Hz sits inside the theta band → the poetic story "the Earth's heartbeat beats in theta; meditating at 7.83 Hz re-attunes you to the planet" (הלב הפועם של כדור הארץ). It powers grounding/earthing narratives, PEMF mats, and countless "Schumann tuning" tracks. Persistent viral myths: "the Schumann resonance is rising to 40 Hz as humanity ascends" — false; the fundamental is geophysically fixed by the cavity's circumference and only wobbles slightly with global lightning activity.

**Claims vs evidence**: playing a 7.83 Hz-modulated *sound* is just theta-band entrainment audio; there is nothing "Schumann" about it physically (you cannot hear 7.83 Hz as a pitch — it's below hearing range; it's used as a beat/pulse rate). No peer-reviewed evidence that 7.83 Hz audio "synchronizes you with Earth" or heals; small studies of weak magnetic 7.8 Hz fields on sleep are preliminary. Grade **D** for Earth-connection claims; **B-/C** as ordinary theta entrainment audio. Honest app framing: "7.83 Hz is a lovely theta-range pulse with a beautiful story; the relaxation comes from the theta rhythm, not from the planet."

## 3. Solfeggio Frequencies (תדרי סולפג'יו)

### 3.1 The real origin story (document this internally, market gently)

- The specific Hz values originate with **Dr. Joseph Puleo** (naturopath, 1970s–90s), who said he was divinely guided to apply **Pythagorean digit reduction to verse numbers in Numbers 7:12–83** (the repeating pattern of the tribal offerings): verses 12, 18, 24… reduce to 3–9–6 → 396; 417; 528; 639; 741; 852. Published with **Dr. Leonard Horowitz** in *Healing Codes for the Biological Apocalypse* (1999). 174, 285, 963 were extrapolated later to complete a 9-tone grid (the 3-6-9 "Tesla numbers" pattern: each frequency's digits sum to 3, 6, or 9). ([RationalWiki](https://rationalwiki.org/wiki/Solfeggio_frequencies), [The Mind Orchestra history](https://www.themindorchestra.com/blog/solfeggio-frequencies-history-truth-origins-healing-sound), [Sound Medicine Academy](https://www.soundmedicineacademy.com/pages/sound-healing-blog/solfeggio-frequencies))
- **Not ancient Gregorian chant.** The *solfège syllables* (Ut-Re-Mi-Fa-Sol-La) are genuinely medieval (Guido of Arezzo, ~11th c., hymn "Ut queant laxis"), but medieval chant had **no standardized Hz pitch at all** — A=440-style standards are 19th–20th century. No historical tuning system ever produced 396/417/528… Hz. The "lost frequencies suppressed by the Church" narrative is modern lore. ([NeuroVIZR](https://neurovizr.com/blogs/garnet-corner/about-the-solfeggio-frequencies), [OmLumi science review](https://omlumi.com/guides/solfeggio-frequencies-what-the-science-shows))
- Also note: the nine tones do not form a musically coherent scale (adjacent intervals are irregular), which is why solfeggio tracks are usually drones/pads at a single frequency.

### 3.3 The 528 Hz literature — what exists and its quality

- **Rat study** (Iranian group, *Genes & Genomics* 2019): 528 Hz at 100 dB, 2 h/day × 21 days → reduced anxiety behaviors, increased brain testosterone, reduced ROS ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13258-018-0753-6)). Note: 100 dB is *hearing-damage-level* exposure; not a model of gentle listening.
- **Cell study** (2017): 528 Hz sound reduced ethanol-induced death in human astrocyte cultures (+20% viability) ([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/319015020_The_Effects_of_528_Hz_Sound_Wave_to_Reduce_Cell_Death_in_Human_Astrocyte_Primary_Cell_Culture_Treated_with_Ethanol)). In-vitro; sound in a dish ≠ music in ears.
- **Small human pilots**: 3-minute 528 Hz music exposure reduced state anxiety vs control (n=48, p=.022) ([REDVET article](https://veterinaria.org/index.php/REDVET/article/download/787/500/)); a Japanese pilot (Akimoto 2018) found reduced salivary cortisol/tension after 528 Hz vs 440 Hz music (n=9).
- **Quality verdict**: tiny samples, no preregistration, no replication by independent groups, implausible mechanism for "DNA repair" (originating from a misreading of a water-clustering/DNA lab anecdote promoted by Horowitz). Grade **C** for "may be mildly relaxing like other pleasant tones," **D** for DNA/miracle claims. App copy should say "מסורתית מכונה תדר האהבה" (traditionally called the love frequency), not assert repair.

## 4. 432 Hz vs 440 Hz (כיוון 432 הרץ)

**The claim**: A4=432 Hz is "mathematically consistent with the universe," "natural," "heart-opening," while 440 Hz is harsh/agitating (and, in conspiracy versions, imposed by Goebbels/Rockefellers to keep humanity anxious).

**The history, honestly**:
- Pitch was chaotic for centuries (A ≈ 409–457 Hz across Europe). **Verdi** did back an 1884 Italian decree for A=432 (to protect opera singers' voices) — that part is real ([Schiller Institute history](https://archive.schillerinstitute.com/music/rev_tuning_hist.html)) — but he framed it as practical standardization, not cosmic healing.
- A=440 emerged bottom-up (US industry/broadcasting used it from the 1920s; NIST's predecessor broadcast 440 from 1936) and was recommended internationally at a **1939 London conference** as a compromise. **The Goebbels/Rockefeller mind-control story is fabricated**; its main modern source is the same **Leonard Horowitz** of solfeggio fame, building on Lyndon LaRouche's 1980s "Verdi Initiative" ([Lead Stories fact-check](https://leadstories.com/hoax-alert/2022/10/fact-check-tuning-standard-of-a-440-hz-was-not-driven-by-nazi-and-rockefeller-desire-for-discord.html), [jakubmarian.com](https://jakubmarian.com/the-432-hz-vs-440-hz-conspiracy-theory/)). Note also: "432 = ancient" fails because ancient/medieval music had no Hz standard, and the "sacred number" arguments depend on arbitrary unit choices (seconds).

**The studies (all small, worth citing carefully)**:

| Study | Design | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Calamassi & Pomponi 2019 ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31031095/)) | Double-blind crossover, n=33 | 432 Hz: HR −4.79 bpm vs 440; small non-significant BP/respiration drops; higher satisfaction |
| Emergency nurses COVID pilot 2022 ([Acta Biomed](https://www.mattioli1885journals.com/index.php/actabiomedica/article/view/12915)) | Double-blind RCT pilot | Lower anxiety/stress with 432 Hz |
| Dental extraction RCT, Brazil ([SciELO](https://www.scielo.br/j/jaos/a/kkzqnX5PgqgdKzT3BhHdX7w/?lang=en)) | RCT, salivary cortisol | 432 Hz cortisol lower than 440 Hz and control |
| Cancer patients 2025 ([BMC Complement Med](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12906-025-04758-5)) | Randomized crossover, 432 vs 443 Hz | Both reduced HR; 432 slightly more (−3 vs −1 bpm median) |

**Honest assessment**: a handful of small trials show slight autonomic differences favoring 432 Hz; mechanism unclear (an 8 Hz retune also slightly changes timbre/brightness — slower, "darker" sound may simply be more relaxing); no large replication. Grade **B-/C**. Perfectly reasonable to offer a 432 Hz music library as a *preference*; do not claim cosmic superiority. The difference is ~a third of a semitone — most listeners cannot reliably tell blind.

## 5. Sound Healing Practices (ריפוי בצליל)

### 5.1 The practice landscape (as taught)

- **Tibetan/Himalayan singing bowls (קערות טיבטיות)**: metal alloy bowls, struck or rimmed; rich inharmonic overtones and slow amplitude beating. Practitioners place bowls on/around the body ("vibrational massage"), speak of clearing blockages and balancing chakras. (Historical note: the "ancient Tibetan healing bowl" lineage is itself thin — bowls as *healing* instruments are largely a 20th-century Western framing.)
- **Crystal (quartz) bowls**: purer, louder, long-sustaining single tones; sold tuned to chakra notes (C root … B crown); practitioners claim quartz "amplifies intention."
- **Gongs / gong baths (אמבטיית גונג)**: dense, evolving inharmonic wash; taught as ego-dissolving, "surrender to the wave of sound."
- **Tuning forks**: unweighted forks near the ears (e.g., solfeggio sets, "Biosonics" body tuners C256/G384); **weighted forks applied to the body**, classically **128 Hz** (Otto 128) placed on the sternum, sacrum, joints, and acupuncture points — the fork is used for its palpable vibration (mechanoreception), somewhat plausible for local relaxation via the same channels as vibroacoustic therapy.
- **Sound baths (אמבטיית צלילים)**: lying-down group listening sessions combining bowls, gongs, chimes ([Cleveland Clinic overview](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/sound-bath), [The Conversation beginner's guide](https://theconversation.com/a-beginners-guide-to-sound-baths-what-they-are-how-to-choose-a-good-one-and-what-the-research-shows-220024)).
- **Chanting/toning**: OM chanting — practitioners often assign OM the pitch **136.1 Hz** ("the Earth-year tone," C#, from Cousto's Cosmic Octave — see §8); in reality people chant OM at whatever pitch their voice sits.
- **Drumming / shamanic drumming (תיפוף שמאני)**: monotonous drumming at ~**4–4.5 beats per second** for "journeying" (Michael Harner's core-shamanism standard: 15–30 min at ~205–220 bpm... i.e., ~3.5–4.5 Hz).

### 5.2 The evidence

| Practice | Key studies | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Singing bowl meditation | **Goldsby 2017** (n=62, observational, *J Evid Based Complementary Altern Med*): significant pre/post drops in tension, anger, fatigue, depressed mood, pain; anxiety ↓; spiritual well-being ↑; **no control group** — expectation, rest, and lying down for an hour are all confounds ([SAGE full text](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/2156587216668109), [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27694559/)). A 2023 Chilean RCT found acute relaxation response vs control ([MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2254-9625/13/2/24)); a 2025 systematic review of singing-bowl clinical studies finds consistent short-term relaxation but weak methodology overall ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2213422025000241)) | **B-** for acute relaxation/mood; **D** for chakra/energetic claims |
| Sound baths/gongs | Mostly small pre/post studies; reviews find reduced subjective stress/anxiety, some HRV improvement; authors uniformly call for RCTs | **C/B-**; safe, pleasant, plausibly relaxation + slow breathing + group ritual |
| Shamanic drumming | Neher 1962 ("auditory driving"); **Maxfield 1990**: theta dominance during 4–4.5 Hz drumming; Harner & Tryon 1996: mood/imagery effects; Szabó/Gingras-era EEG studies: alpha-theta increases, though expectation moderates results ([EEG responses to shamanic drumming, 2016](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:942601/FULLTEXT01.pdf), [follow-up study](https://openaccesspub.org/sleep-and-sleep-disorder-research/article/eeg-responses-to-shamanic-drumming-does-the-suggestion-of-trance-state-moderate-the-strength-of-frequency-components-671)). Separately, **group drumming** has decent RCT evidence for depression/anxiety/inflammation (Fancourt 2016) | **B-** — the *most* relevant "real" theta-audio tradition for a theta app: a 4.5 Hz drum track is both authentic to practice and consistent with the (contested) entrainment literature |
| OM chanting | fMRI (Kalyani 2011): limbic deactivation similar to vagus-nerve stimulation; HRV studies show parasympathetic shift; 2024 RCT of Bhramari+OM: improved wound healing markers, ↓LF/HF ([PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11636174/), [cardiovascular oscillation study](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9127736/)) | **B** — but mechanism is slow breathing + vocal vibration + attention, not the number 136.1 |
| Weighted tuning forks / vibroacoustics | Vibroacoustic therapy (Skille, Norway, 1968→; 30–120 Hz through transducers): small trials in fibromyalgia/pain, 40 Hz most used, 20–45 min sessions ([Skille & Wigram 1989](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/135945758900300202), [evidence roundup](https://vibroacousticsolutions.com/blogs/vibroacoustic-therapy-frequencies/40-hz-vibroacoustic-therapy-evidence-based-research)) | **C/B-** for pain/relaxation via mechanoreception; fork-on-meridian claims **D** |

**Key honest framing**: across sound healing, the *nonspecific* package (slowing down, lying still, long exhalations, expectancy, ritual, beautiful sound masking rumination) is powerfully relaxing and worth delivering; evidence for *frequency-specific* effects is where the science thins out.

## 6. The Legit Evidence Core: Music Therapy & Nature Sounds

Use this as the app's scientific backbone — it's the strongest material you can truthfully cite.

- **Cochrane, music for depression** (Aalbers 2017): music therapy + treatment-as-usual beats TAU alone for depressive symptoms and anxiety, short term; moderate-quality evidence ([Cochrane Library](https://www.cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.CD004517.pub3/full)).
- **Cochrane, music for preoperative anxiety** (Bradt et al.): music listening reduces pre-op anxiety roughly comparably to midazolam in some trials. 2025 multilevel meta-analysis in *eClinicalMedicine* confirms music therapy reduces anxiety broadly ([Lancet eClinicalMedicine](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/eclinm/article/PIIS2589-5370(25)00225-1/fulltext)).
- **Sleep**: Cochrane (Jespersen 2022) — music listening improves subjective sleep quality in insomnia; sedative music = slow tempo (~**60–80 bpm**), low dynamics, stable rhythm. The famous "60 bpm entrains the resting heart" is a heuristic, not a law, but slow-tempo selection is genuinely evidence-aligned. Grade **A-** for music-and-sleep overall.
- **Music + depression meta-analysis** ([BJPsych Open](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/bjpsych-open/article/music-therapy-for-patients-with-depression-systematic-review-and-metaanalysis-of-randomised-controlled-trials/AD9B45562D2499DCCE0D4C4B10C2287C)); **hypertension meta-analysis** ([PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10077636/)).
- **Nature sounds**: **Buxton et al. 2021, PNAS** meta-analysis (36 papers): improved health/affect outcomes, decreased stress and pain; **water sounds best for tranquility, birdsong best for stress/annoyance relief** ([PNAS](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2013097118)); 2024 meta-analyses agree ([Stress journal](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10253890.2024.2402519)). Grade **A-**. For a Hebrew audience: צלילי מים, ציפורים בבוקר, רוח — legitimately therapeutic content categories.

## 7. ThetaHealing × Sound/Frequency (תטא הילינג)

- **The core premise**: Vianna Stibal (1995, Idaho) teaches that the practitioner drops into a **theta brainwave state** (4–7 Hz), goes "up to the Seventh Plane / Creator of All That Is," commands/witnesses healing, and reprograms limiting beliefs (עבודת אמונות). The technique's *name itself* is a brainwave claim — which makes theta-band audio a natural companion product. (Note internally: EEG validation that practitioners are actually in theta is essentially absent; and ThetaHealing as a healing modality has no clinical evidence base — frame app content as meditation support, not treatment.)
- **The Seven Planes of Existence** (Stibal, *Seven Planes of Existence*, Hay House 2016): 1st minerals/crystals; 2nd plants; 3rd animals & humans; 4th spirits/ancestors; 5th angels/masters; **6th the Laws**; 7th All That Is ([book](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/604393/seven-planes-of-existence-by-vianna-stibal/), [ThetaHealing planes course](https://www.thetahealing.com/course-37.html)).

Practitioners cite this to explain why solfeggio/frequency music "belongs" in ThetaHealing practice, while noting Vianna's teaching that Sixth-Plane tools work through the laws and that the ThetaHealer aims *above* them, at the Seventh Plane (i.e., in her system, tone-healing is real but is a lower-plane modality than Creator work). There is no official Vianna-endorsed "ThetaHealing frequency"; the community defaults to: **theta 4–7 Hz beats, 432 Hz tuning, 528 Hz drones, 7.83 Hz Schumann pulses**.

## 9. Safety (בטיחות) — build these into the product

1. **Hearing / volume**: WHO safe-listening limit — **80 dB for 40 h/week** (adults; 75 dB for children); every +3 dB halves safe time ([WHO Q&A](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/deafness-and-hearing-loss-safe-listening)). Practical app rules: default to moderate output, "60/60 rule" tip (≤60% volume, breaks after 60 min), remind users entrainment **does not work better louder**. Long sleep tracks: recommend speaker playback or volume-limited comfortable level; avoid in-ear buds all night.
2. **Epilepsy**: photosensitive seizures are a real risk mainly for **flashing light** (audio-visual entrainment devices, flicker apps) — any AVE/light-pulse feature needs an epilepsy warning and medical-consult gate. Pure-audio rhythmic stimulation: no documented seizure inductions in controlled studies, but standard industry practice is a caution notice for people with seizure disorders ([binauralbeatsmeditation.com guidance](https://www.binauralbeatsmeditation.com/binaural-beats-epilepsy-seizures/), [narrative review on rhythmic stimulation in epilepsy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12787044/)). Musicogenic epilepsy exists but is ~1 in 10 million.
3. **Tinnitus / hyperacusis (טינטון)**: pure-tone drones and beating tones can be aversive or temporarily worsen perception for tinnitus/hyperacusis users; advise low volume, broadband nature-sound alternatives, stop-if-uncomfortable ([safety roundup](https://calmingmusicrelaxation.com/guides/are-binaural-beats-safe.html)).
4. **Not while driving / operating machinery**: any track designed for theta/delta states must carry a "לא בזמן נהיגה" warning — this is universal industry practice for hypnosis/entrainment audio.
5. **Other cautions**: pacemaker/serious cardiac conditions — relevant mainly for *vibroacoustic* (body-transducer) devices, not ordinary listening; pregnancy & young children — no evidence of harm from gentle audio, but keep it to soft music/nature sounds, skip intensive entrainment; psychosis/dissociative conditions — deep trance audio and long sound baths can be destabilizing; standard "not a substitute for medical/psychological care" disclaimer, especially important since ThetaHealing marketing has a history of overclaiming cures.
6. **Session hygiene defaults**: 15–30 min sessions; comfortable seated/lying position; fade-outs and gentle "return" cues at the end of theta/delta tracks; hydration/grounding suggestions after long sound baths (practice-world convention users will expect).
